1. The Problem with Web2 Creator Tipping

If you are a writer on Substack, an open-source maintainer on GitHub, or an artist on X, rewarding you with money is surprisingly difficult. Traditional platforms like Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee, and PayPal take 5% to 15% in platform cuts, charge high minimum foreign exchange fees, and are completely blocked in dozens of countries.

Worse, tipping a small amount like $1 or $2 is economically impossible when credit card processing fees swallow $0.30 + 2.9% of every single donation.

2. Enter Moony: Fixed-Supply Currency on Solana

Moony is an independent digital currency designed specifically as an open monetary public good on Solana.

  • Fixed Supply: Mathematical scarcity without arbitrary inflation or central foundation manipulation.
  • Sub-Cent Fees: Operating on Solana means a micro-tip costs a fraction of a cent in network fees.
  • Permissionless: Anyone anywhere in the world with a smartphone can send or receive Moony instantly.

4. Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Tipping a Creator in 3 Clicks

1 Tap or Scan the Tip Card: Click the creator's Flipcash link or scan their QR code with any mobile camera.

2 Enter Your Moony Amount: Whether it's 10 Moony or 500 Moony, there are no percentage penalties on micro-donations.

3 Instant On-Chain Arrival: Finality on Solana takes under 500 milliseconds. 100% of the funds land directly in the creator's custody.

5. Why This Changes Independent Monetization

When fans can send micro-rewards directly with zero friction and zero platform censorship, creators gain true financial autonomy. Moony and Flipcash remove the middleman and put the value back into the hands of those who create it.
